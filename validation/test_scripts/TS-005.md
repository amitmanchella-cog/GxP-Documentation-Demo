## 1. Purpose and scope
This script shows how SST 3.5 (Draft) handles result amendment (`PATCH /api/results/{id}`, `POST /results/{id}/amend`) and second-person verification (`POST /api/results/{id}/verify`, `POST /results/{id}/verify`). It covers the following GxP findings posted on PR #6: blocking F1–F7 and F9, advisory F12, and F8 for the scripted-test and boundary-test parts. Detailed checks of audit-trail content belong to TS-006. Findings F10 (CR impact wording) and F11 (e-signature decision) are QA/Change Owner actions, so they are out of scope here.
