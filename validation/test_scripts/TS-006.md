# TS-006 — Audit-trail verification for result amendment and verification (URS-002/003/006/010/011, FS-002/003/006/009/010)

Purpose: the audit-trail verification required by SOP-DI-004 §6 (a)–(d) for any change that adds a write path to GxP records, applied to the CHG-4127 amendment (`PATCH /api/results/{id}`, `POST /results/{id}/amend`, FS-009) and verification (`POST /api/results/{id}/verify`, `POST /results/{id}/verify`, FS-010) paths. Also covers preservation of the original value (SOP-DI-004 §5.2) and 21 CFR 11.10(e) (secure, computer-generated, time-stamped audit trail recording operator entries and actions without obscuring previously recorded information). Change-specific functional and rounding cases are in TS-005.
